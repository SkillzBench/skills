# blog-style

[![SkillzBench](https://skillzbench.com/api/badge/blog-style-79b3f9)](https://skillzbench.com/skills/blog-style-79b3f9)

Measure writing-style statistics (sentence length, vocabulary, transitions, phrasing) from 5 to 10 posts you or your client wrote, and produce a voice profile for VOICE.md, blog-persona, and blog-write. Use for "learn my style", "build a writing baseline". It describes style; it does not detect or attribute authorship, and it is not for scoring a post (blog-analyze) or writing brand rules (blog-brand).
